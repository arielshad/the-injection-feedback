# The Injection: feedback

Public feedback for [The Injection](https://theinjection.dev), a feed of AI security news and research that is sourced, credited and explained.

## How to reach us

- **A story is wrong** (severity, affected versions, CVE, who deserves credit): [open a correction](https://github.com/arielshad/the-injection-feedback/issues/new?template=correction.yml).
- **Research or an advisory we should cover**: [suggest a story](https://github.com/arielshad/the-injection-feedback/issues/new?template=story-tip.yml). It must be public, from a primary source.
- **Questions, ideas, anything else**: [start a discussion](https://github.com/arielshad/the-injection-feedback/discussions).

Corrections are reviewed and applied in a later update, on the same story URL.

## Not here

Please do not report vulnerabilities in third-party products here. Report them to the vendor first; we cover them once they are public.

## For agents and developers

Data formats are documented at [theinjection.dev/developers](https://theinjection.dev/developers/) and [theinjection.dev/llms.txt](https://theinjection.dev/llms.txt).
